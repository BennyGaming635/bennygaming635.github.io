Ninja Vault

Privacy Policy

Passwords belong to you. Here is how Ninja Vault handles your information.

Effective and last updated: 16 October 2026
Developer: Benjamin Graetz · Android app: com.bennygaming635.ninja

At a glance

Ninja Vault 2.1 for Android is a local password manager. Your vault is encrypted on your device. There is no Ninja Vault account, automatic cloud sync, advertising, or usage analytics. The Android release has no Internet permission.

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes Ninja Vault 2.1 for Android, including its optional device-transfer and encrypted-backup features, and this privacy-policy page. For privacy questions, contact Benjamin Graetz at rjhj8647@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, authenticator setup keys, backup passphrases, or vault files in a support message.

2. Information stored and why

Ninja Vault processes the information you choose to enter: login titles, usernames, passwords, notes, website addresses, collections, favorites, and optional authenticator setup keys. This information is used to manage your vault, search and organize entries, identify weak or reused passwords locally, and generate authenticator codes on your device.

Vault entries are encrypted in app-private storage. Display and sorting preferences are stored locally. Generated passwords and passphrases remain on your device unless you save, copy, or transfer them. We do not receive your vault contents.

3. Device authentication and security

Opening the vault uses Android's device authentication, such as a fingerprint or screen-lock credential. Android handles biometric templates and authentication credentials; Ninja Vault does not obtain or store them. The app locks when it goes into the background and blocks ordinary Android screenshots of its screens.

Encryption and device protections reduce risk but cannot guarantee security on a compromised device. There is no developer-operated recovery service, and we cannot recover your backup passphrase or a deleted vault.

4. Optional camera and QR transfer

Camera permission is requested only when you choose to start scanning, after an in-app explanation. Camera frames are processed locally to read QR codes; Ninja Vault does not save or upload them. The scanner model is bundled for offline use. You can deny or revoke camera permission in Android settings and continue using the vault.

When you initiate a transfer, the selected vault contents are encrypted for the paired receiving device using fresh pairing keys and authenticated encryption. The transfer uses displayed and scanned QR codes, with no Ninja Vault server. Pairing expires after ten minutes, and the receiving user must compare the verification code and confirm before importing. Only transfer to a device you trust. Imported copies remain on the receiving device until deleted there.

5. Optional encrypted backups

Backups are created only at your request. The exported vault file is encrypted using a separate passphrase you choose. You select its location through Android's system file picker, which may include a cloud or other file provider. That provider processes the encrypted file under its own privacy policy. Ninja Vault does not automatically upload backups or enable Android automatic app backup or device-transfer copies.

Restoring a backup reads the file you select and decrypts it locally using the passphrase you provide. Exported backup files persist until you delete them from their chosen locations. Keep the passphrase separate and secure; the developer cannot reset it.

6. Clipboard and sharing you choose

Copying a password, username, generated secret, or authenticator code places it on the system clipboard, where Android and apps with permitted clipboard access may process it. Ninja Vault attempts to clear copied content after 30 seconds while it is running, unless newer clipboard content has replaced it. Clearing cannot be guaranteed if the app stops running. Manage any clipboard history or device clipboard-sync feature separately.

We do not sell your information or share vault data with advertisers or analytics services. Copies you deliberately transfer, export, or paste are controlled by you and the recipients or providers you select.

7. Retention and deletion

Vault information remains on your device until you delete entries, erase the vault through Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. These actions do not delete exported files, clipboard history, or copies on other devices. Delete those separately, including any cloud-provider trash or version history.

No Ninja Vault account exists to delete. Because the developer does not hold your vault, deletion and access to vault contents are controlled on your device. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the message you send, use them to handle your request, and retain correspondence only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of that correspondence using the contact address above.

8. This website and third-party services

This policy page adds no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to serve and protect the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this page does not send your vault contents to GitHub.

Android, Google Play, email services, and file providers have their own privacy practices for the services you use. Their processing is separate from Ninja Vault's local vault processing.

9. Children and policy changes

Ninja Vault does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated account or backend. If a child sends personal information in a support message, contact us to request deletion.

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or data handling. The current policy and its effective date will remain available on this page. Material changes will also be explained in the app or release information as appropriate.
