# Customise your website

Visit https://bennygaming635.github.io/customise.html or click **customise** at the bottom left of your website.

1. Edit your name, introduction, interests, projects, skills, favourite games, and social links.
2. Click **Save & preview** to see changes. Drafts appear only with `?preview=1` in your current browser; visitors see the published version.
3. Click **Download content.js** to save your updated content.
4. Open https://github.com/BennyGaming635/bennygaming635.github.io, choose **Add file → Upload files**, upload the downloaded file as `content.js`, and commit to `main`.
5. GitHub Pages publishes the change automatically. Allow a minute or two, then refresh.

Each category has editable entries with a title, subtitle, description, link, and animated object. You can add or remove entries. Use full `https://` URLs or `mailto:` addresses. A blank link still opens a local detail view. Set a portrait image URL to replace your GitHub avatar. Your avatar appears in an animated metallic frame.

**Restore published content** resets your draft. The editor is a local preview tool, so visitors cannot change the public website through it. Publishing requires access to your GitHub repository.

On desktop: left/right arrows change category, down enters or advances through items, up returns to categories, and Enter opens an item. You can also click icons, use the mouse wheel, or swipe. On mobile, the website becomes a scrolling page with animated object shelves.

The site has no build step or trackers. Your custom project, skill, game, and email objects are original procedural 3D models rendered locally in canvas. They animate when selected and respect reduced-motion preferences. The category icons and font reference https://www.kelindi.ca/. Existing pages under `ninja/` are retained. The Work section has been removed.
